BCI Lead Hunter

Build a relevant project search around the packages you sell.

1. Select an application

Which lead should I work first?

1. Confirmed open package: Relevant doors/hardware, a quotation or buying deadline, and a known buyer. Contact the contractor QS/procurement or appointed door specialist first.

2. Newly appointed contractor / early construction: Ask whether the door package is unawarded, being priced, approved, ordered or installed. A main-contract win does not establish package buying status.

3. Tender stage: Find bidding main contractors or specialists and offer schedule/quotation support. Ask when pricing is needed and who wins the supply decision; a quote is not an order.

4. Concept / design / documentation: Contact the architect or developer for design intent, specification and approved-brand requirements. Build the relationship early; revenue may be further away.

5. Occupied buildings / refurbishment: Contact the property/facilities manager for replacement issues and budgets, then coordinate with their maintenance contractor.

Search tender and non-tender projects. Procurement route alone does not tell you whether the door package is open. Older projects remain worth checking when there is a relevant package, but deprioritise confirmed placed orders unless there is a genuine additional need.

Quick qualification questions

  • Does the scope include doors, hardware, seals, automation or sliding systems?
  • Who specifies, approves and purchases this package?
  • Has the supplier order been placed? What remains open?
  • When are quotes, approval and delivery required?
  • Which brands are specified, and can alternatives be considered?

Start broad with “door”. Try separate searches if BCI does not support OR. Narrow by package terms after checking scope; descriptions can omit the door package.
